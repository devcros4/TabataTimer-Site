Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Transparent and simple.

Last updated: January 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Tabata Timer+ ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. Tabata Timer+ is designed to function primarily on your device with minimal data collection.

2.1 Personal Data

We do not collect personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number unless you voluntarily provide it to us via support channels.

2.2 Usage Data

We may collect anonymous usage data to help us improve the app's performance and stability. This includes crash reports and aggregated usage statistics that cannot be traced back to individual users.

3. Health Kit Integration

If you choose to integrate Tabata Timer+ with Apple HealthKit, the app will request permission to save workout data (such as calories burned and duration) to your Health app. This data is handled entirely on your device and is governed by Apple's HealthKit privacy policies. We do not store this health data on our servers.

4. Data Storage

Your workout history and preferences are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, this data may be lost unless you have backed up your device.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics or payment processing (Apple App Store). These services have their own privacy policies.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@tabatatimer.app.
